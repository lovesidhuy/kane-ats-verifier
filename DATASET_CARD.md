# Dataset Card: JobsFarm × Kane CLI Verified Browser Trajectories

> *"Every green run becomes training data for browser agents."*

This dataset contains **reward-filtered, cryptographically verified browser-agent form-filling trajectories** generated by running **JobsFarm** (`lovesidhuy/jobsfarm`, AGPL-3.0) through **TestMu AI Kane CLI** (`@testmuai/kane-cli` v0.10.4).

## 2. Automatic Grader & Reward-Filtering Methodology

Unlike unverified web-scraping or raw Playwright recordings, **only runs that pass Kane CLI's automatic grader** are exported:
1. **Tier 1 (Live Public ATS):** Chrome navigates to live no-login public postings (`job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5183044008`, `jobs.lever.co/leverdemo`), fills fields, asserts DOM values, and aborts before Submit.
2. **Tier 2 (Isolated Dry-Run Sandbox):** `tests/kane/harness.py` wires 10 synthetic personas × 7 ATS layouts × 4 variants (`standard`, `mutated_ids`, `strict_policy_screening`, `dirty_dedup_batch`) to JobsFarm's untouched `DOMAutofillEngine`, `resolve_answer()`, and `deduplicate()`.
3. **Cryptographic Gate:** Every `.evidence` pack is validated locally via `kane-cli evidence validate` (`evidence: valid (L1, status finalized)`). **Pass/fail for grading uses `test_md_done.overall_status`**, not validate alone. Failed or unverified runs are excluded from the export.
4. **Closed-loop trajectories:** `evidence/loop_runs/cl2_visa_seed_live/` (agent policy fix, seed still planted) and replay fixtures in `tests/fixtures/ndjson/`.

## 3. JSONL Schema & Redacted Sample Row

Each line in `verified_trajectories_*.jsonl` is a JSON object with:
- `source_type`: `"kane_testmd_evidence"` or `"kane_explore_lease"`
- `evidence_pack_id`: Source `.evidence` filename or explore lease ID
- `verification_tier`: `"tier1_live_public_ats"` or `"tier2_isolated_dry_run_harness"`
- `ats_platform`: `"greenhouse" | "lever" | "ashby" | "bamboohr" | "indeed" | "linkedin" | "workopolis" | "qa_bank" | "discovery_pipeline"`
- `persona_id`: One of 10 synthetic candidate personas (`senior_fullstack`, `new_grad`, `visa_sponsorship`, `healthcare_ops`, `devops_contractor`, `security_analyst`, `data_engineer`, `product_manager`, `qa_automation`, `international_coop`)
- `dom_variant`: `"standard" | "mutated_ids" | "strict_policy_screening" | "dirty_dedup_batch"`
- `input_dom_fields`: Scanned DOM field identifiers (or `"live_public_ats_dom"`)
- `qa_policy_context`: Deterministic policy constraints (`work_auth`, `sponsorship`, `salary_expectation`, `clearance`)
- `verified_injected_actions`: Ordered step list (`step`, `action_type`, `instruction`, `status`, `locators`, `output_value`)
- `evidence_status`: `"valid_L1_passed"` or `"valid_explore_completed"`
